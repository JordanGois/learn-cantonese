The Framework

Three Shelves, Three Movements

All six tones fit onto three pitch shelves:

  • High shelf: T1 sits here flat. T2 rises up to here.
  • Mid shelf: T3 sits here flat. T4 starts here and falls. T5 rises up to here.
  • Low shelf: T6 sits here flat. T4 falls down to here. T5 starts here.

Three tones are level (T1, T3, T6). Three tones move (T2 rises, T4 falls, T5 rises). Learn the shelves first, then the movements.

Tricky Pairs

T1 vs T3 — Same shape, different height. Both are flat and level. T1 is on the high shelf; T3 is on the mid shelf. Compare: saam1 (three) vs sei3 (four). If you can hum a high note and a mid note, you can tell these apart.
T2 vs T5 — Same direction, different starting point. Both rise. T2 starts at mid and climbs to high. T5 starts at low and climbs to mid. Compare: gau2 (nine) vs nei5 (you). T2 sounds brighter and more energetic; T5 sounds gentler.
T4 vs T6 — Both are low, but one moves. T4 starts mid and falls to low. T6 is already low and stays there. Compare: si4 (time) vs ji6 (two). T4 has a sighing quality; T6 just sits.
