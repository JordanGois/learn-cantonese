Cantonese Glossary

All vocabulary from the course, organized by topic

Romanization

Jyutping
The standard romanization system for Cantonese. Uses letters a-z for sounds plus a number 1-6 for tone. Example: nei5 = consonant "n" + vowel "ei" + tone 5.
Tone number
The digit 1-6 at the end of a Jyutping syllable. It tells you the pitch pattern to use when speaking. Without the correct tone, you say a different word entirely.

Tone Concepts

Tone 1 — High Level --- ---
High, flat pitch. Like singing a steady "la!" Anchor word: saam1 (三, three).
Tone 2 — High Rising ╱
Starts mid, rises to high. Like asking "Really?" Anchor word: gau2 (九, nine).
Tone 3 — Mid Level --- ---
Middle pitch, flat. Your normal speaking voice. Anchor word: sei3 (四, four).
Tone 4 — Low Falling ╲
Starts mid, drops low. Like sighing "oh..." Anchor word: si4 (時, time).
Tone 5 — Low Rising ╱
Starts low, rises to mid. A soft, curious "hmm?" Anchor word: nei5 (你, you).
Tone 6 — Low Level --- ---
Low, flat pitch. A bored mumble. Anchor word: ji6 (二, two).
