Lesson 2

Three Shelves

Hear the level tones — then learn to tell them apart

In Lesson 1 you met all six Cantonese tones. Today we zoom in on the three level tones — the ones where your pitch stays steady, no rise or fall. Think of them as three shelves on a wall: one high, one in the middle, one low.

If you can hear which shelf a syllable sits on, you already have half the tone system sorted. The other three tones (rising and falling) will come later.

Hear The Three Shelves

Click each button to hear a pure tone at that shelf's pitch. Listen for the height difference — all three are flat and steady, but they sit at different levels.

T1 — High shelf
T3 — Mid shelf
T6 — Low shelf

Hear Real Words

Now hear the shelves in actual Cantonese words. Each word sits on one shelf and stays there.

三
saam1
three — high shelf anchor
Bright, high, and flat. This is your reference point for T1.
四
sei3
four — mid shelf anchor
Relaxed, middle pitch. Your normal speaking voice probably lives here.
二
ji6
two — low shelf anchor
Low and flat, like the bottom of a sigh. This is your T6 reference.
食
sik6
to eat — low shelf
Same low flat pitch as ji6. Both sit on the bottom shelf.
心
sam1
heart — high shelf
Same bright high pitch as saam1. Both sit on the top shelf.

Ear Training: Name That Shelf

A random level tone will play. Your job is to say which shelf it sits on. Try to get 5 in a row.

Sort The Words

Click a word to select it, then click the shelf it belongs on. When all words are placed, press Check.

Recall Quiz

Practice With Your Partner

Shelf drill

  1. Say saam1 (three), sei3 (four), ji6 (two) one after the other. Try to make the pitch clearly different each time.
  2. Ask your partner to say a number (1-4) in Cantonese and see if you can tell which shelf it sits on.
  3. When eating dinner, try saying sik6 (eat) on the right low shelf. Your partner will tell you if it sounds right.

The three shelves are the foundation. Once you can hear high vs mid vs low, the rising and falling tones are just movement between shelves.

What's Next

In Lesson 3: Rising Tones, we add movement. You will learn T2 (high rising) and T5 (low rising) — tones that travel between shelves instead of sitting still on one.
