Lesson 9

Family Words

The words you'll need when meeting your partner's family

Cantonese family words are more specific than English. There is no generic "brother" or "sister" — Cantonese distinguishes older vs. younger for every sibling. This specificity reflects how important family hierarchy is in Cantonese culture. Getting the right term is not optional — it is built into the language.

Parents

爸爸
baa4 baa1
Dad
T4+T1. The "ba" sound is similar to English!
媽媽
maa4 maa1
Mom
T4+T1. Same tone pattern as baa4 baa1.

Siblings

In English, "brother" covers both older and younger. In Cantonese, you must specify older or younger. Using the wrong term would be like mixing up someone's name.

哥哥
go1 go1
Older brother
T1+T1. Both high level.
姐姐
ze2 ze1
Older sister
T2+T1. Rise then high.
弟弟
dai6 dai2
Younger brother
T6+T2.
妹妹
mui4 mui2
Younger sister
T4+T2.

Partner Terms

老公
lou5 gung1
Husband
T5+T1. Literally "old male."
老婆
lou5 po4
Wife
T5+T4. Literally "old woman." (Not rude in Cantonese!)
男朋友
naam4 pang4 jau5
Boyfriend
naam4 = male, pang4 jau5 = friend.
女朋友
neoi5 pang4 jau5
Girlfriend
neoi5 = female.

Combining with Lesson 7

Remember hai6 (to be) and ge3 (possessive particle) from Lesson 7? Now you can use them with family words to introduce people:

佢係我嘅媽媽
keoi5 hai6 ngo5 ge3 maa4 maa1
She is my mom
佢係我嘅老公
keoi5 hai6 ngo5 ge3 lou5 gung1
He is my husband
佢係我嘅老婆
keoi5 hai6 ngo5 ge3 lou5 po4
She is my wife

Practice With Your Partner

  • Learn your partner's family members' Cantonese titles. Which sibling terms apply to their siblings?
  • Practice introducing each one: "keoi5 hai6 ngo5 ge3 [relation]".
  • Ask your partner: are there any other family terms they use often? (Uncle, aunt, grandparent terms can come later — master these core ones first.)
