Lesson 8

Numbers 1–10

Count in Cantonese — and drill your tones at the same time

Numbers are the best tone drill in Cantonese. Every number has a different tone, so counting 1–10 exercises almost all six tones. You already know what the numbers mean, so you can focus entirely on getting the tones right.

Tone Patterns in Numbers

Look at which tones appear in the basic numbers:

  • T1 (high level): 1, 3, 7 — three of them!
  • T2 (high rising): 9 — just one
  • T3 (mid level): 4, 8 — two
  • T5 (low rising): 5 — just one
  • T6 (low level): 2, 6, 10 — three of them!

No T4 (low falling) in the basic numbers! The high and low level tones dominate. When you count, you are bouncing between the top and bottom shelves with a few stops in the middle.

Number Dictation

A number will play. Listen to the tone and pick which number it is. Try to get 5 in a row!

Sort Numbers by Tone

Click a number, then click the tone shelf where it belongs.

Practice With Your Partner

  • Count 1–10 out loud with your partner. Focus on hitting the tones.
  • Have your partner say a random number — repeat it back with the correct tone.
  • Exchange phone numbers in Cantonese, one digit at a time.
  • Count objects around the house: jat1, ji6, saam1...
