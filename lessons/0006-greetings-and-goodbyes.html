Lesson 6

Greetings & Goodbyes

Beyond nei5 hou2 — starting and ending conversations

You know nei5 hou2 (hello) from Lesson 1. Now learn more ways to greet people and say goodbye — the bookends of every conversation with your partner's family.

This is the first lesson in Phase 2. From here on, you are building real conversational vocabulary.

New Greetings

早晨
zou2 san4
Good morning
zou2 = early (T2 high rising), san4 = morning (T4 low falling). Use when you wake up or arrive in the morning.
晚安
maan5 on1
Goodnight
maan5 = evening (T5 low rising), on1 = peace (T1 high level). Say this before bed.
你好嗎？
nei5 hou2 maa3?
How are you?
The maa3 particle turns any statement into a yes/no question. You already know nei5 hou2 — just add maa3!
我幾好
ngo5 gei2 hou2
I'm quite good
The response to nei5 hou2 maa3. gei2 = quite (T2 high rising). ngo5 (T5) + gei2 (T2) + hou2 (T2) — a rising pattern.

Goodbyes

Bye bye
baai1 baai3
Bye bye
Casual, borrowed from English but with Cantonese tones! baai1 (T1 high level) then baai3 (T3 mid level). High to mid — easy.
再見
joi3 gin3
See you again
More formal. joi3 = again (T3), gin3 = see (T3). Both mid-level — easy!

The maa3 Particle

This is one of the simplest and most useful grammar patterns in Cantonese:

  • Any statement + maa3 = yes/no question
  • nei5 hou2 (You're good) → nei5 hou2 maa3? (Are you good?)

That is the entire pattern. No word reordering, no verb changes. Just add maa3 at the end. You will see this particle everywhere.
